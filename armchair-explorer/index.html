How to Use the Explorer

This application is a Universal Context Engine. It allows you to break down any complex topic into its component parts and view them through different analytical lenses.

1. Enter a Topic

Start broadly. The system works best with:

  • Locations: Paris, Kyoto, The Amazon Rainforest
  • Historical Events: WWII, The Renaissance, Apollo 11
  • Concepts: Stoicism, Quantum Mechanics, Jazz

2. Explore Sub-Categories

Once the topic loads, you will automatically be shown a list of categories (e.g., Neighborhoods, Battles, Theories). Select one to drill down.

3. Choose a Lens

Examine your selected category through three distinct perspectives:

  • Details: Facts, figures, and notable features.
  • Analysis: Cultural impact, significance, and deeper meaning.
  • Context: History, origins, and background information.

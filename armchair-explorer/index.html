About City Explorer

City Explorer is designed for armchair travelers—people who love learning about cities without necessarily planning a trip.

Exploration Tiers

  • Foundations - What is this place? History, geography, architecture
  • Life - How do people live? Food, neighborhoods, customs
  • Legacy - What has this place created? Arts, music, literature
  • Discovery - What's hidden? Secrets, transformations, symbols

About City Explorer

City Explorer is designed for armchair travelers—people who love learning about cities without necessarily planning a trip. Discover the stories, history, and visual beauty of cities around the world from the comfort of your chair.

How It Works

  1. Enter any city name (e.g., "Paris", "Kyoto", "Istanbul")
  2. Click OK to load the city
  3. Choose a tier to explore different aspects
  4. Click any tab within that tier to dive deeper

Exploration Tiers

  • Foundations - What is this place? History and architecture
  • Life - How do people live here? Food and daily customs
  • Legacy - What has this place created? Arts and literature
  • Discovery - What's hidden or unique? Secrets and scenes

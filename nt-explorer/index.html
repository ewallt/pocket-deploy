About NT Threads

NT Threads helps you discover how Scripture weaves together. Enter any Bible passage and explore meaningful cross-references that illuminate its themes, language, and theology.

How It Works

  1. Enter a Bible passage (e.g., "John 3:16" or "Romans 8:28-39")
  2. Click OK to retrieve the passage text
  3. Click any tab to explore different aspects of the passage

What You'll Find

  • Cross References - Related passages with connections explained
  • Theological Themes - Major doctrines present in the text
  • Historical Context - Background that illuminates meaning
  • Literary Structure - How the passage is organized
  • Greek Lexicon - Word-by-word Greek analysis
  • JFB Commentary - Insights from Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

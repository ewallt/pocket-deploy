About NT Threads

NT Threads helps you discover how Scripture weaves together. Enter any Bible passage and explore meaningful cross-references that illuminate its themes, language, and theology.

How It Works

  1. Enter a Bible passage (e.g., "John 3:16" or "Romans 8:28-39")
  2. Click the "Cross References" tab
  3. Explore 6-8 passages that connect through shared themes, language, or theology

What You'll Find

Each cross-reference includes:

  • The reference - Book, chapter, and verse
  • The connection - How it relates to your passage
  • The text - The actual verse for context

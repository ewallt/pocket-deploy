Welcome

Welcome to the Cross Perspectives Explorer. This tool helps you explore diverse theological perspectives on the cross and atonement through AI-guided discovery.

How to Use

  • Enter a query: Type a theologian's name (Moltmann, Girard), a theological concept (wrath, scapegoat), or a biblical figure (Apostle Paul)
  • Get suggestions: Click the 💡 icon to receive AI-generated category suggestions based on the knowledge base and theological scholarship
  • Select & explore: Choose a category to analyze it through three analytical lenses

The Three Lenses

  • The Insight — Direct synthesized understanding of the topic
  • The Voices — Which theologians address this and their key contributions
  • The Synthesis — Implications, connections, and theological contrasts

Knowledge Base

This tool draws from a curated database of insights from theologians including Graham Maxwell, René Girard, Jürgen Moltmann, Greg Boyd, N.T. Wright, Ellen G. White, and many others who explore non-violent, restorative views of atonement.
